Networks Day

December, Department of Mathematics, Uppsala University

A one-day event to gather those working on networks in the Stockholm and Uppsala region, and nearby.

We will feature talks from researchers at UU, KTH and SU. Topics from pure graph theory to applied network science; e.g. random process on graphs, random graphs/probabilistic networks, temporal graphs, algorithms for detecting structure in networks.

The event will be from 10-16.00 in Ångström Laboratory, Uppsala. Coffee and tea from 9.30.
