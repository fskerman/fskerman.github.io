Networks Day 2026

Wed 2nd December, Ångström Laboratory, Uppsala University

A one-day event to gather those working on networks / graphs in the Stockholm and Uppsala region, and nearby.

Confirmed Speakers: Angela Fontan (KTH), Anda Skeja (UU), Ece Calikus (UU) and Mason Porter (Nordita / SU). Further speakers and titles and abstracts to follow.

The event will be from Wed 2nd December 10.00-16.00 room 80101 (map) in Ångström Laboratory, Uppsala University. Coffee and tea from 9.30 just outside room 80101. There is no cost and no need to RSVP for the day, all are welcome to join for lunch we will organise nearby (at own expense). To join for dinner RSVP to Fiona by Mon 30th Nov.

All welcome!

This meeting will cover topics from pure graph theory to applied network science; e.g. random process on graphs, random graphs/probabilistic networks, temporal graphs, algorithms for detecting structure in networks. The aim is to gather researchers in these topics from the Uppsala/Stockholm region and perhaps further afield. This is hopefully the first in a series of meetings which we plan to rotate between SU / Nordita, KTH and UU.

Organisers: Fiona Skerman (UU) with Ioana O. Bercea (KTH), Maria Deijfen (SU), Matteo Magnani (UU) and Mason Porter (Nordita / SU). Please contact Fiona to be added to a mailing list.
